## 🧑‍💻 Short bio

I'm a Senior Frontend Engineer with more than 8 years of experience building and leading reliable, high-impact web applications, from SaaS platforms and e-commerce to banking, airline and insurance products. I care about strong architecture, solid end-to-end testing and a sharp eye for detail, and I enjoy turning complex requirements into dependable, user-centric solutions.

Day to day I work with React, Next.js and TypeScript, backed by Node.js and AWS. I've led frontend teams, mentored developers through code reviews and pairing, migrated legacy platforms to modern server-side rendered architectures, and moved design systems to accessible, component-driven foundations. Performance, SEO and accessibility are part of how I define "done".

## 🤖 Building with AI

I'm genuinely enthusiastic about using artificial intelligence to create digital products. I bring it into the whole building process, from exploring ideas and prototyping to writing, reviewing and testing code, so I can move faster without lowering the quality bar. For me AI is a creative partner, not a shortcut: the architecture, the decisions and the final result are still mine to own.
